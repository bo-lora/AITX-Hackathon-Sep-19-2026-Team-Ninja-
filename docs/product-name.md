# Product name: ContextNinja

Plays on **contextual inquiry**: a researcher observes engaged users in their natural environment to uncover deep-seated pain points and hidden workarounds they might never think to mention.

The product is that ninja: it watches the operator **in situ** (Chrome extension recording DOM and task flow) and extracts the workflow into a downloadable skill.

Do not market it as “watches a video.” Video is a poorer, flaky stand-in for being in the room. The inquiry happens in her real browser; Grok only sees the packaged skill at the end.
